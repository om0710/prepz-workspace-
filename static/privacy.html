BU PREPZ - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026 | Application: BU PREPZ (https://om123bansal-prepz-app.hf.space)

1. Introduction

Welcome to BU PREPZ ("we", "our", or "us"). BU PREPZ is an AI-powered college exam preparation assistant and academic workspace designed to help students study course materials, syllabus topics, and past exam questions efficiently.

2. Information We Collect via Google OAuth

When you log in to BU PREPZ using Google Sign-In, we only request access to basic, non-sensitive profile information:

  • Full Name: Used to personalize your study dashboard.
  • Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier and to send study notifications/welcome emails.
  • Profile Picture URL: Used solely to display your user avatar in the workspace interface.

We do NOT access your Google Drive, Contacts, Gmail inbox, or any sensitive private account data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Authenticate your login and maintain secure sessions.
  • Track your study streaks, contribution scores, and mock test progress.
  • Enable AI chat sessions with syllabus course materials and study document indexing.
  • Send essential service emails (e.g., account confirmation).

4. Data Sharing and Protection

  • No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data with any third parties or advertising networks.
  • Security: All communication between your browser and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/SSL. OAuth tokens are verified securely following Google's official security standards.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

You may request the deletion of your account and associated study data at any time by contacting us. You can also revoke BU PREPZ's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Permissions Settings.
